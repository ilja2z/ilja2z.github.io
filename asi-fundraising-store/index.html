ESP+ Stores · Fundraising storefront

A clickable prototype of the buyer flow: a parent or student lands on a school fundraising store, picks a product, and checks out. Every step shows how much goes to the cause. Built from the Cosmos tokens and components in the case’s Figma file.

Things to try

  • Pick a colour on the sweatshirt: the photo, thumbnails and summary follow.
  • Type a name on back or choose 2XL: the price summary and the amount for the fund update as you go.
  • Add to cart without a size (Mistakes and fixes): the error says what to do.
  • Remove an item in the cart, then undo it from the toast.
  • Leave a 4-digit ZIP and continue: inline validation explains the fix.
  • Place the order with card 4000 0000 0000 0002: a declined payment, and nothing is lost.

Design decisions

  • The cause sits next to the products. Raised amount, supporters and days left are above the fold, and the countdown bar makes the close date hard to miss.
  • The contribution is visible at every step: product card, price summary, cart, checkout and confirmation, so the cause stays part of every decision.
  • No surprises at checkout. The price summary updates as you choose, and the order summary stays on screen through all four steps.
  • Errors say how to fix them, with an icon and words, never colour alone. Selected chips get a check, not only a colour.
  • One primary action per screen. Back and edit are quiet ghost buttons.

How it’s built

Screens 1, 2 and 4 follow the Figma frames 05–07. The cart, payment, review and confirmation were designed straight in code from the same Cosmos components: Button, Chip, Input, Control, Badge, Progress bar and Toast. Colours, spacing and radii are the Figma variables, as CSS custom properties.
