From a hotel in the feed to a booked room

Mabrook is a Dubai super app: video, shopping, food and hotels behind one login. This is the stay flow in one path: the feed, the hotel, dates and guests, room options, checkout, and what happens when a payment fails or the guest needs a few more minutes.

Things to try

  • Tap Book now on the resort in the feed: stays sit in the same full-screen feed as video, food and shop.
  • Change the dates: tap a start and an end day. Nights and every price after it update.
  • Add a child in Rooms and guests: an age row opens only when it’s needed.
  • Swipe the price plans under a room: flexible or saver, and the checkout follows your choice.
  • Leave the name empty and complete the booking: the field says what’s missing.

Design decisions

  • One detail template for stays, food and goods: gallery, rating, dates and guests at the top, then facilities.
  • A sheet, not a new page, for dates and guests, so the hotel stays in view.
  • Steppers instead of dropdowns. Children and pets open their own rows only when needed.
  • The deadline first. The free-cancellation date leads the checkout, before any form.
  • Hold & resume keeps the room for 15 minutes, so it isn’t lost mid-checkout.

How it’s built

Screens 1–8 follow the Figma Flow 1, with photos, icons and colours taken from the file. The loading, error and declined-payment states come from its Under the hood page. The booked confirmation was designed in code from the same components.
