Goal Tracker is an open-source platform by Data Act Lab (Stockholm) that helps governments and citizens track progress on the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

National platforms run with statistics offices in Somalia, Tanzania, South Africa, Togo, Sweden, Guinea, Chad and Ethiopia, with partners such as UNDP, Sida and the World Bank.

Valor’s first non-profit work was with Gapminder on Dollar Street, the photo-and-data project Bill Gates called “the most complete picture of humanity I’ve seen yet”. I supported that project, and its success brought Alexandra Silfverstolpe Tolstoy, founder of Data Act Lab, to Valor.

Lead Product Designer, working directly with the founder in 2022 and 2023, together with a designer-illustrator and the development team.

  1. Information architectureFrameworks, goals, targets and indicators, plus clear data gaps where numbers are missing.
  2. Visualisation systemFor large, uneven datasets: rankings, maps, trend lines, finance flows and scorecards, all on the 17-colour SDG palette.
  3. Country portalsTanzania and Somalia, built with their national statistics offices.
  4. National reviewDesign of Tanzania’s 2023 Voluntary National Review, including analysis of how the war in Ukraine hit food and grain supply in African countries.
  5. GuidelinesDocumentation so country teams could keep publishing on their own.

Clear data helps governments and international funds see where progress stalls and where the money should go.
