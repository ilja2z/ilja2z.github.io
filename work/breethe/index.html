Breethe

A meditation and sleep app with 18M+ downloads. We helped new users find their first session faster, moved breathing to Apple Watch and added an AI coach.

Breethe has 2,500+ meditations, sleep stories, hypnotherapy tracks and breathing tools. Many people signed up or started a subscription and then never really used the app: faced with thousands of tracks, they didn’t know where to begin, drifted away and cancelled.

The goal was to show every new user, in the first minutes, what Breethe can do for them specifically.

Lead Product Designer on the Valor Software team working with Breethe. I worked directly with Suren Kocharian, VP Production & Operations, from user interviews and flows to handoff and post-release analysis.

Our team covered personalised onboarding, the Apple Watch app, the AI coach, the Android version and a shared component library.

  1. Why are you here?People pick their main reason first.
  2. What can we help with?Sleep, stress, anxiety or learning to meditate.
  3. Top goalsGoals shape the recommended plan.
  4. Nightly nudgeReminders that support the plan.
  5. Free trial, after the planThe trial comes once the value is clear.

Separate flows for different needs, so someone who can’t fall asleep and someone dealing with anxiety start in different places, with a first session that matches their answers.

A series of user interviews showed that breathing exercises dropped in the middle of the day: people were busy at work and their phone wasn’t at hand. The answer wasn’t another feature in the phone app but a new place for the practice.

The Apple Watch app starts a short guided session in one tap, no phone needed, right where the midday dip was.

An AI coach section with eight coach personalities, from sleep and emotional support to parenting and relationships, each with its own colour. People chat by text or voice, agree to clear terms before the first chat, and get guided to a matching meditation or practice.

We designed the Android version and optimised the structure and animations across the app, so navigation and playback feel smoother on both phone platforms.

A shared component library covers iPhone, Android and Apple Watch: 12 colour tokens, one type family and components with every state.

  • −32% onboarding drop-off after personalised onboarding launched.
  • +16% trial-to-paid conversion.
  • +52% daytime breathing sessions after the Apple Watch release.
  • One design language across iPhone, Android and Apple Watch.
