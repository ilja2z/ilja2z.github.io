Breethe

A meditation and sleep app with 18M+ downloads. A short engagement focused on its personalised onboarding, so new users find a first session that fits them.

Role
Product Designer
Scope
Personalised onboarding, including its web version in Webflow
Client
Worked directly with the VP Production & Operations
Engagement
Short project

Breethe has 2,500+ meditations, sleep stories, hypnotherapy tracks and breathing tools. Many people signed up or started a subscription and then never really used the app: faced with thousands of tracks, they didn’t know where to begin, drifted away and cancelled.

The goal was to show every new user, in the first minutes, what Breethe can do for them specifically.

Product Designer on the Valor Software team working with Breethe. In a short engagement I worked directly with Suren Kocharian, VP Production & Operations, on the personalised onboarding: the flow steps, the personalisation behind the questions, and the web version of the onboarding built in Webflow.

Other areas of the app were handled by the wider team and are not part of this case.

  1. Why are you here?People pick their main reason first.
  2. What can we help with?Sleep, stress, anxiety or learning to meditate.
  3. Top goalsGoals shape the recommended plan.
  4. Nightly nudgeReminders that support the plan.
  5. Free trial, after the planThe trial comes once the value is clear.

Separate flows for different needs, so someone who can’t fall asleep and someone dealing with anxiety start in different places, with a first session that matches their answers.
