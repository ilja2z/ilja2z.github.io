Studytube

A skill-based learning platform for teams. I rebuilt its design system from the ground up and helped redesign onboarding so more new users reach their first lesson.

Studytube helps organisations develop the skills their teams need through personalised learning, structured content and data-driven development. Two problems stood in the way:

  • The design foundation was inconsistent, which slowed delivery for the multiple teams working across the platform.
  • A significant drop-off after registration: only 43% of new users reached their first lesson.

I joined an existing UI design team and took ownership of rebuilding Studytube’s design system from the ground up, while designing and improving selected product features and flows.

I worked in a cross-functional design group of four product designers and a UX researcher.

  • Created and maintained the Studytube design system.
  • Defined foundations: colour tokens, typography and component patterns.
  • Rebuilt and improved core components and interaction states.
  • Established documentation and usage guidelines.
  • Supported adoption across multiple product teams, so LMS, LXP and authoring share one language.

Together we ran user interviews, analysed product behaviour and reviewed key funnel metrics. We mapped the onboarding journey, found the main friction points and designed a new flow that helps users understand the product and reach their first learning experience faster.

  1. RegistrationCreates the entry point.
  2. Goal selectionReveals learner intent.
  3. Personalised pathsConnect intent to content.
  4. First lessonDelivers the first value moment.

We also simplified the authoring flow so content teams can move from idea to publish with confidence: brief, content, knowledge check and publish, with a live preview of the lesson.

  • First-lesson conversion grew from 43% to 59%: +16 percentage points, about 37% relative growth.
  • One consistent design system adopted across multiple product teams.

The work combined design-system ownership with product design, research and measurable activation improvements. studytube.com
