What I changed from the 2024 design

  • Statuses you can trust. “Rejected” showed a green tick. Each state now has its own colour, icon and next action.
  • Contrast. Green and grey labels were about 1.6:1. All text now passes WCAG AA, including the disabled button.
  • The photo stays visible. The camera button no longer covers the document, so you can see if the shot is sharp.
  • Plain words. “Accordingly to local bylaws” → “By law, we need…”. “Need an assistance?” → “Need help?”.
  • Unhappy paths. Glare, blur, an unclear field, an expired licence and manual entry each have a designed state.
