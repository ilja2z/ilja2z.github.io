A field app that works before, and without, the signal

Two flows from the Logistics Cluster mobile app. The first launch downloads offline data while a short tour runs, then sets a country in focus. A local partner reports a blocked road from an old Android with no signal, and the report waits on the phone until the network returns.

Things to try

  • Tick the Privacy Policy and continue. The download starts there, and the tour runs while it loads. Drag the card or tap the dots.
  • Pick a country to focus on. Next step only turns on once there is a focus, and skipping is always one tap away.
  • Follow a few activities and search the list. Home opens already tailored.
  • Submit a report with the signal off. It is saved as Unsent. Turn the signal on and send all unsent.
  • Close a report halfway with the cross: it offers a draft instead of losing the work.

Design decisions

  • The wait teaches the app. The first download can take minutes on a field connection, so the tour runs on top of it.
  • Optional, but useful. Focus and follow are two short steps that can be skipped and changed later.
  • Taps, not typing. Big tiles and icon lists work with gloves, in any language, on a 360 px screen.
  • Offline is a normal state. The app says it is offline, compresses the report and queues it with a clear status.
  • One flow, two tiers. Lite and Full share the report flow and the queue. Try Submit report on Home.

How it’s built

Screens 01–22 follow Flow 1 and Flow 2 in the Figma file, with the icons, photos and colours taken from it. The tour cards are exported from the file. The country picker, the photo preview, the location card, the draft prompt and the sending states were designed in code from the same components.
