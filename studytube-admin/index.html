Studytube admin · Find who’s falling behind and act

A clickable prototype of an admin flow: an L&D manager spots the people at risk of missing their learning deadlines, nudges them in bulk, fixes a failed invite, then opens one person’s learning and budget. Built from the Studytube design-system components in the case’s Figma file.

Things to try

  • Click the At risk card. The status cards are filters, and the search says what it searches.
  • Tick a few people and send a reminder from the bulk bar. Add Cody Fisher to see why he gets skipped.
  • Fix Cody’s failed invite. The badge names the problem, and the fix is one click away.
  • Search for someone who isn’t there. The empty state shows the way out.
  • Open Jenny Wilson and extend her overdue course, then watch the cards above the table update.
  • On Budget & transactions, release a reservation or move a paid course to the team budget, then undo.

Design decisions

  • Status first. Four cards answer “who needs me?” before the table does, and they double as filters.
  • Actions show up where the decision is made: bulk actions when rows are selected, row actions in the row menu.
  • Errors carry the fix. A failed invite says what’s wrong with the address and opens the fix in place.
  • Every status has an icon and a label, never colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
  • Money is explained, not just shown: spent, reserved and refunded sit next to the balance, and a note says where refunds go.

How it’s built

Screens follow the Figma frames 01–03 of Flow 1 and the table states: no results, loading and error. Dialogs, menus and toasts were designed straight in code from the same components: Status filter card, Search bar, Chip, Table, Badge, Tabs, KPI card, Progress bar and Button. Colours, spacing and radii are the Figma variables in the Studytube mode, as CSS custom properties.
